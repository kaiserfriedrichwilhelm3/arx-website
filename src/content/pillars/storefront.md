## What it is

The practice's public-facing site, rebuilt around a single objective: take a person who has been told to call this office and get them to a booked appointment. Editorial register, accessible by default, no dark patterns, no chat widget. The Storefront is where Voice and Intake meet the patient for the first time.

## How it is configured

Built per practice from a shared template. Brand, photography, and copy are voiced jointly with the provider. Schedule integration runs through the same configuration as Voice — one source of truth for hours, providers, and accepted insurance.

## What "complete" looks like

Complete means: a sub-second time-to-interactive on mobile; one primary action above the fold; insurance and intake forms that take less than three minutes; full keyboard and screen-reader support; analytics restricted to first-party event counts, no third-party tracking pixels.

## Status in the reference deployment

In deployment. The Cespedes Cardiology Storefront is the first build; rolling out alongside Voice in the next deployment window.
