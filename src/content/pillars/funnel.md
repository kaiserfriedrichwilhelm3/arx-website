## What it is

The Funnel is the practice's operating dashboard for everything that happens between visits — outbound referrals waiting on a return note, internal referrals from the primary care network, recall reminders for chronic patients, and the no-show queue. Voice and Intake feed it; the provider works against it.

## How it is configured

Referral sources, recall intervals, and no-show policy are configured per practice and per specialty. The system never auto-contacts a patient outside of the practice's own policy. Cadence rules are written down, reviewed by the provider, and version-controlled.

## What "complete" looks like

Complete means: every referral has a state and an owner; recall outreach runs on the cadence the provider chose, not a vendor default; the dashboard reads as one screen, not a series of vendor portals; the practice manager can answer "where does this patient stand" in under five seconds.

## Status in the reference deployment

In deployment. Building the dashboard in parallel with Voice; first integration target is the referral return-note workflow.
